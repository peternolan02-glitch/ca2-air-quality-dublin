Air Quality in Dublin

Air quality in major cities is an important issue. In Dublin, air quality has been a topic of discussion for many years because it can have negative effects on public health across the city. This website explores the main causes of air pollution, why it matters to the city and its people, the data used to measure it, and some practical solutions to the issue.

Why Air Quality Matters

Air quality matters because it affects people’s health and the environment. Poor air quality can lead to breathing problems and other health issues, especially for children, older people, and those with existing medical conditions.

What This Website Covers

This website looks at the main causes of air pollution in Dublin, the effects it can have on daily life, and the ways it can be improved in the future.
