Solutions to Improve Air Quality in Dublin

Improving air quality in Dublin will take both individual action and government support. Since pollution comes from different sources, the solutions also need to work in different areas of daily life.

One important solution is reducing traffic emissions. This can be done by improving public transport, encouraging cycling and walking, and helping more people switch to electric vehicles.

Another solution is reducing the use of solid fuels such as coal, peat, and wet wood in home heating. Cleaner heating systems and better energy-efficient homes could help lower the amount of harmful particles released into the air.

Better planning in cities can also make a difference. More green spaces, less traffic congestion, and stronger environmental rules can all help create a cleaner and healthier city for everyone.

  • Use more public transport, cycling, and walking
  • Encourage cleaner and electric vehicles
  • Reduce the use of solid fuels in homes
  • Improve green spaces and city planning
